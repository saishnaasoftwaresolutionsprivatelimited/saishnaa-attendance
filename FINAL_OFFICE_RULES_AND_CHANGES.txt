SAISHNAA ATTENDANCE SYSTEM - FINAL OFFICE RULES

Office timing: 09:40 AM to 05:40 PM

Lunch break: 30 minutes
Weekly off: Sunday
Late window: 09:41 AM to 10:10 AM
Monthly late allowance: 2
Monthly 1-hour permission: 1 (available after the two monthly late allowances are used)
Casual Leave: 1 CL credited monthly; unused CL carries forward without expiry
Marriage Leave: 10 days
Approved OD: Paid working day
Paid leave: Paid day
Unpaid leave/LOP: Unpaid day
Holiday: Paid day
Sunday: Paid day
Absent past working day: Full-day salary deduction
Half-Day LOP: 50% salary deduction
Attendance correction: Check-In / Check-Out / Both and applicable leave issues; request must be submitted within the next day and approved
Future dates: Not included in salary earnings, deductions or projections
Overtime: Removed from attendance/payroll calculation
Allowed late: No direct salary deduction; escalation follows company attendance rules
